Everything you need when something breaks

Belegora takes care of the tedious parts: collecting receipts, suggesting details and reminding you before your dates.

Reminders before your dates

Warranty, returns, insurance, maintenance: Belegora shows a Windows notification as many days ahead as you like.

Phone upload via QR code

Scan the QR code and upload a photo in your browser. The receipt arrives in your inbox over your Wi-Fi. No cable, cloud or phone app.

Text recognition

Purchase date, amount and retailer are suggested from the receipt. Review, save, and the device is created in one click.

Warranty traffic light

The device list shows at a glance where a warranty ends soon, with a “Warranty until” column.

Command palette Ctrl+K

Find any device by name, serial number, retailer, tag, file or borrower, plus actions such as the PDF or a backup – with arrow keys and Enter.

Batch capture

Many receipts one after another: receipt on the left, short form with suggestions on the right. Enter saves, Ctrl+→ skips.

Maintenance log with costs

Record repairs, services, spare parts and accessories with their cost. Follow-up and total cost per device, also in the PDF and as CSV.

Lending list and weekly overview

Who has the drill? Lent to, since when, planned return – with a reminder and history. Plus an overview once a week.

Insurance PDF in 10 languages

After water damage or a burglary: a report with cover page, list by room, totals, photos and receipt images. Choose any report language.

Import from Excel and CSV

Bring your existing list with column mapping. Export to CSV, and dates to ICS for any calendar.

Automatic backups

Backups including attachments run automatically. Restore shows a preview of what comes back.

Offline, no account or subscription

No cloud, no account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your PC.

Good to know: Belegora stores your details and dates and does not provide legal advice. Notifications appear while Belegora is running; it can optionally start with Windows. Text recognition uses Windows OCR and needs the matching Windows language pack.

Frequently asked questions

Who can see my data?

Only you. Belegora sends no data to us or anyone else; there is no account and no telemetry. Phone upload works only within your local network.

Where is my data stored?

Locally on your PC in the app data folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\MinhStudios\Belegora), together with copies of your attachments and the automatic backups.

How do I move to a new PC?

Create a backup in Belegora (a ZIP with all data and attachments), copy it to the new PC, install Belegora there and restore the backup. The preview shows what will be restored.

Does Belegora check my warranty claims?

No. Belegora stores your details and dates and does not provide legal advice. Whether you have a claim is up to the retailer, manufacturer or insurer.

Do notifications appear when the app is closed?

Notifications appear while Belegora is running; it can optionally start with Windows. You can also export dates as ICS to your calendar.
