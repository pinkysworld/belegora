Your data stays with you

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The app collects no data

Minh Nguyen Studios receives no device records, receipts, photos or usage data through Belegora. There is no account, advertising, telemetry, analytics or cloud sync.

Local storage

Your entries, copies of attachments and automatic backups are stored locally in the app data folder on your PC. Belegora does not encrypt the archive; protect your Windows account and drive. You delete data, backups and exports yourself on your device.

Phone upload

For phone upload, Belegora starts a small receiving service on request, for at most 15 minutes and only in your local network. It accepts connections from private network addresses only and requires a random one-time key (token) contained in the QR code. Only PDF, JPG and PNG files are accepted; they go straight to the inbox folder on your PC. Nothing is sent over the internet or to us.

Text recognition

Text recognition uses the OCR built into Windows and runs entirely on your PC. Receipt images are not uploaded.

Exports and other apps

PDF, CSV and ICS exports contain the details you select; you decide whom you share them with. Opening attachments is subject to your default app, purchasing to the Microsoft Store.

This website

These static pages use no cookies, trackers, external scripts or fonts. The host, GitHub Pages, processes connection data needed to serve the site, such as your IP address (see the GitHub privacy statement).

Contact

If you email us, we process your message only to answer it. Please do not send receipts, serial numbers or backup files. Controller: Michel Nguyen, Minh Nguyen Studios, Windmühlenstr. 74, 07937 Zeulenroda-Triebes, Germany.
